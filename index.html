How it works

The loop is the whole product

Five steps, in this order, every time. There is no path that skips your approval.

  1. 1

    Write privately

    Say it however you need to. This side is teal: only you and Moderator see it.

  2. 2

    Moderator reads the record

    Your agreement, shared calendar, and past messages become the facts it can cite.

  3. 3

    A draft is proposed

    One topic, one request, one date. Make it shorter, firmer, or add context.

  4. 4

    You approve it

    Nothing is transmitted until you press Approve & Send. You stay in control of the words.

  5. 5

    It joins the shared record

    Delivered, timestamped, and no longer editable after it is shared.

Teal is private

Private with Moderator

Raw input, analysis, and unapproved drafts live here. The other participant cannot see this screen, see that you opened it, or see what you deleted.

Periwinkle is shared

The shared record

Approved messages, confirmed events, and document sources. Both participants see the same information, in the same order, with the same timestamps.

Safety by design

Some messages don’t get sent

Every draft is validated before delivery. A personal attack, threat, or demand that contradicts the agreement is stopped, named, and rewritten so the request survives without the attack. There is no bypass.

  • Received messages can be reported in two taps.
  • Restrict Communication limits contact without deleting the record.
  • Safety Center is reachable from every screen.
