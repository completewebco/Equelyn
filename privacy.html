Privacy

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Launch note: This page covers the public marketing website. It must be reviewed and expanded to match the final iOS app’s data practices before App Store submission.

Information you provide

If you join the launch list, Equelyn receives the email address you submit. We use it only to send the Moderator launch link or respond to a request you make.

Information collected automatically

This static website does not set advertising cookies. Standard server logs may record technical information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time for security and operational purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that host the website, deliver email, or protect the service, subject to their agreements with Equelyn. We may also disclose information when legally required.

Retention

Launch-list information is retained until the launch message is sent or you ask us to remove it. Operational server logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for security and troubleshooting.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete information submitted through this website by contacting hello@equelyn.com.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This marketing website is not directed to children, and the launch list is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this notice as the service develops. The current version will be posted here with its effective date.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to hello@equelyn.com.
